## Brand & Style

The design system marries classic print broadsheet heritage with contemporary digital intelligence publishing. Built for serious editorial discourse, analytical investigative reporting, and real-time dispatches, it targets an audience of discerning readers, researchers, policy analysts, and journalists who value substance, legibility, and architectural rigor over ephemeral UI trends.

The visual direction draws heavily from the International Typographic Style (Swiss Modernism) grounded upon tactile, broadsheet physical metaphors:
- **Heritage Broadsheet Architecture**: Disciplined multi-column newsprint grids, authentic masthead headers complete with volume numbers, edition cycles, meteorological notations, and issue dates.
- **Hairline Ink Structure**: Distinctive 1px borders replicating mechanical lead rules and cut-lines, creating crisp physical separation across story blocks without drop shadows or artificial elevation.
- **Subtle Modernism**: Deep ink-black typography set against warm, tactile newsprint canvas tones, punctuated by restrained emerald green signals for breaking intelligence badges and live telemetry.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in unbleached rag paper, letterpress ink, and vintage archival printing, modernised by an alert emerald for live data points.

### Primary Color
- `#141414` (Deep Printer's Ink): Used for prominent editorial headlines, mastheads, high-contrast borders, and primary interactive elements. Provides commanding visual weight without the synthetic harshness of pure black (`#000000`).

### Secondary & Accent Colors
- `#059669` (Intelligence Emerald): Reserved for live feeds, breaking intelligence badges, active market tickers, and status indicators. It delivers an authoritative signal while harmonising with warm paper tones.
- `#8C6A3E` (Aged Sepia / Editorial Ochre): Used for section categorisation, op-ed rubrics, publication metadata, and secondary journalistic emphasis.

### Canvas & Structural Neutrals
- `#F7F4EC` (Warm Newsprint / Kraft Wash): The default surface across the entire broadsheet canvas, reducing ocular fatigue and imbuing the interface with physical paper warmth.
- `#EDE7DB` (Tonal Paper Tint): Secondary surface used for callout boxes, sidebar registers, masthead sub-bars, and data tables.
- `#D8D2C2` (Sepia Rule / Hairline Border): The standard 1px structural ink dividing line that carves out column gutters, editorial compartments, and cell boundaries.
- `#54504A` (Charcoal Carbon): Body typography, journalistic bylines, photo captions, and subheadings.

## Typography

The typography pairs traditional literary editorial craft with utilitarian modernist labels.

- **Editorial Body & Headlines (Newsreader)**: Evokes the optical scaling and legibility of vintage hot-metal and phototypesetting newspaper presses. Headlines display tight line-heights mimicking compact lead typesetting. Long-form article text breathes through balanced proportional leading (`22px` - `26px`).
- **Metadata, Telemetry & Section Indicators (Space Grotesk)**: Geometric, technical, and condensed in rhythm. Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) for issue dates, wire timestamps, edition numbers, bylines, and category tags.
- **Numerals & Indexes**: Ranked list items (e.g., "01", "02") utilise tabular lining numerals set in `Space Grotesk` or italicised `Newsreader` to ensure visual alignment in density columns.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an authentic broadsheet multi-column structural grid, strictly disciplined by physical rules:

- **Desktop (12-Column Editorial Grid)**: Elements span 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 columns. Articles sit adjacent across continuous hairline dividers. Masthead and breaking tickers occupy full 12-column ribbons.
- **Tablet (6-Column Grid)**: Secondary sidebars tuck beneath focal stories or collapse into horizontal tabular strips. Margins scale to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (Single to 2-Column Grid)**: Content stacks sequentially with vertical hierarchy preserved. Lead stories occupy full width, with quick-read wire feeds displayed in compact two-column index rows.
- **Dividers & Gutters**: Content blocks do not float in open space; they are compartmentalised by continuous `1px` borders (`#D8D2C2` or `#141414`), reminiscent of newsprint mechanical cuts and rule markers. Padding within cells maintains a strict `1rem` or `1.5rem` breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews modern blur filters, glassmorphic sheen, and diffused drop shadows in favor of authentic **Print Structural Flatness**:

- **Zero Ambient Shadows**: Visual hierarchy and stacking depth are produced exclusively through linework, tonal paper contrast, and typographical scale.
- **1px Hairline Ink Dividers**: Structural boundaries use solid `1px` rules in `#D8D2C2`. Critical sections or featured lead packages are framed in stark `1px` or `2px` `#141414` black outlines.
- **Tonal Inset Panels**: Depth within sub-features, intelligence dossiers, and stock tickers is established by transitioning the container background from `#F7F4EC` to `#EDE7DB`.
- **Active & Pressed States**: Interactive buttons and modules do not float up with elevation; they invert (reverse ink: black background with kraft text) or display a solid offset 1px rule.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a strict **Sharp (`0`)** shape language.

- **Right Angles Throughout**: Every card, news compartment, image frame, input container, and masthead block uses crisp `0px` border-radius.
- **Utility Badges & Pills**: Status pills (such as `#059669` LIVE indicators or edition tags) maintain sharp 90-degree corners, preserving the look of genuine stamped letterpress tags and Linotype column slugs.
- **Imagery**: Photographs and documentary plates must never be rounded or clipped into circles. They are set in razor-sharp rectangular frames with optional `1px` ink borders and italicised caption gutters underneath.

## Components

### Masthead & Edition Bar
- **Top Bar**: Hairline-divided row featuring date, volume number, edition location (e.g., "Vol. XLIV No. 12 • Global Edition"), live weather summary, and exchange rates set in `label-sm`.
- **Main Masthead**: Centered prominent blackletter/serif publication logotype, flanked by secondary intelligence tickers or quick dispatch access links.
- **Category Navigation**: Horizontal strip with `1px` top and bottom ink borders. Links formatted in `Space Grotesk` uppercase (`label-md`), divided by subtle middle dots (`•`) or vertical rules.

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#141414` fill, `#F7F4EC` text, sharp `0px` corners, uppercase `Space Grotesk` text (`label-md`). Hover transitions to `#2C2B29`.
- **Secondary Action**: Transparent background with a `1px` solid `#141414` border, `#141414` text. Inverts to solid black on hover.
- **Intelligence Signal Button**: Solid `#059669` fill with white text for active live updates, audio dispatch play triggers, or urgent dispatches.

### Editorial Cards & News Compartments
- **Lead Feature Block**: Enclosed in a full `1px` solid ink border. Features a high-contrast `Newsreader` headline (`headline-xl`), metadata byline with publication timestamp, single or multi-column body lead-in, and full-bleed crisp photography.
- **List & Column Modules**: Numbered intelligence lists (e.g., "Popular / Wire Dispatches") featuring `01`, `02`, `03` in large tabular lining typography separated by horizontal hairline dividers.

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- **Status Badge (Live / Breaking)**: Sharp rectangular badge, `#059669` background with white text, or hollow with a glowing green signal dot.
- **Topic Slug**: Crisp uppercase `#EDE7DB` background with `#54504A` text, bordered by `1px` `#D8D2C2`.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- Minimalist open or boxed inputs with `1px` `#D8D2C2` outline, shifting to `#141414` on focus. No inner shadow. Accompanied by monospace placeholder text and clean serif entry text.

### Data Tables & Intelligence Registers
- Dense, hairline-grid tabular registers for market indexes, commodities, and legislative monitors. Alternating rows with `#EDE7DB` tonal fills and right-aligned tabular numerals.
