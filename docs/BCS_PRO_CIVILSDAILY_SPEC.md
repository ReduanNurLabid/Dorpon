## Detailed Section Breakdown

### 1. Syllabus Breadcrumb & Microtheme
- **Purpose**: Instantly anchor the news to the official BPSC syllabus.
- **Format**: `Subject › Microtheme`
- **Examples**:
  - `Bangladesh Affairs (Paper V) › Macroeconomy, Trade & Foreign Reserves`
  - `Bangladesh Affairs (Paper V) › The Constitution, Organs of State & Writ Jurisdiction`
  - `International Affairs (Paper VI) › Bilateral Diplomacy, Treaties & Regional Geopolitics`
  - `General Science & ICT (Paper VII) › Cybersecurity, Digital Governance & Public Infrastructure`

### 2. Why in the News
- **Purpose**: Factual anchor. 2 sentences explaining exactly what happened today without jargon or filler.

### 3. Question-Led Analytical Breakdown
- **Purpose**: Answer direct analytical questions with bold-anchored points.
- **Rule**: Every bullet must begin with a bold topic anchor (`**Point Title:** Details...`).
- **Core Questions to Answer**:
  - *What is the policy / incident?*
  - *What are the economic/administrative implications?*
  - *Constitutional & Statutory Framework* (exact Articles of the Bangladesh Constitution & statutory Acts).

### 4. Hard Data & Telemetry
- **Purpose**: Memorizable statistics for written exam papers.
- **Sources**: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS), Bangladesh Bank (BB), Ministry of Finance, World Bank, IMF.

### 5. Challenges & Structural Bottlenecks
- **Purpose**: 3 numbered, high-yield points identifying institutional or structural weaknesses.

### 6. Way Forward (Exam Answer Goldmine)
- **Purpose**: 3 actionable, pragmatic policy recommendations formatted for 15-mark written answer conclusions.

### 7. Matching Past Year Question (PYQ)
- **Purpose**: Explicitly link today's news to a real BPSC Preliminary or Written question from the past 30 years.
- **Format**: `[Exam Batch, Subject, Marks] Exact Question Text`
